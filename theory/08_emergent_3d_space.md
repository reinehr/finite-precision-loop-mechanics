# Emergent 3D Space

This mechanism does not begin with space as a container.

It begins with:

```text
processes, relations, coupling strengths, potential gradients
```

Space is interpreted as the large-scale ordering of stable relations between loop traps.

---

## No Background Space At The Bottom

Below stable loop structures, there is no reason to assume ordinary left/right/up/down geometry.

The primitive relation is not:

```text
object A is at position x
```

but rather:

```text
process A influences process B with coupling strength w
```

Only after many stable loops coexist can their relation network be rendered as geometry.

---

## Potential Pressure

If nodes and loops have finite capacity, then excess potential cannot be absorbed indefinitely.

When a local process saturates:

```text
excess potential -> displaced into weaker/farther relations
```

This creates pressure-like behavior in the network.

Space, in this picture, is not where potential moves. Space is the effective structure created by how potential can move.

---

## Why Three Dimensions?

The proposed 3D argument combines two pressures:

1. potential must be able to disperse;
2. stable structures must remain possible.

In one and two dimensions, random walks are recurrent: they return with probability 1. Potential has difficulty escaping its origin cleanly.

In three dimensions, random walks become transient: potential can disperse.

In four or more dimensions, dispersion is easier, but stable knots and bound structures become less natural. Knots can be undone in higher-dimensional spaces, and orbit stability becomes more fragile.

The conjecture:

```text
3D is the lowest dimension that allows robust potential dispersion
and the highest dimension that preserves stable loop/knot-like structure.
```

---

## Space Between Loops, Not Inside Loops

A loop trap is internally process-like, not spatial in the ordinary sense.

Space emerges between stable loops:

```text
loop A <-> relation structure <-> loop B
```

This also reframes distance:

```text
distance = effective relational separation,
not primitive emptiness between objects
```

---

## Relation To Light

A purely linear light-like process does not need pre-existing space.

It is an update chain:

```text
state -> next relation -> next relation
```

Ordinary spatial propagation appears only after stable loop structures define a relational background. In that sense, light is more fundamental than space in this mechanism.

---

## Testable Direction

The 3D idea needs simulation.

Useful tests:

1. Build random relational networks with finite capacity and potential pressure.
2. Let coupling strengths adapt under residue transfer.
3. Measure effective dimension from graph statistics.
4. Check whether dimension near 3 is an attractor.
5. Check whether dimensions above or below 3 destabilize loop traps.

Until then, emergent 3D space is a candidate consequence of the mechanism, not a result.
