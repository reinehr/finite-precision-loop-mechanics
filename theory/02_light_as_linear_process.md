# Light As Linear Process

The simplest process in this framework is not matter. It is light-like propagation.

The minimal light-like step is:

```text
z_in -> w * z_in -> z_out
```

There is one input, one output, and no local self-reference.

---

## Why Use `Z[i]`?

The state is represented as a Gaussian integer:

```text
z = a + bi
```

This is the smallest integer-like structure with two orthogonal components. It is a natural finite test bed for phase-like behavior:

- multiplication by `i` rotates by 90 degrees;
- real and imaginary parts can play the role of two field quadratures;
- phase is arithmetic, not an added decoration.

This does not prove that physical light literally lives in `Z[i]/p`. It explains why `Z[i]/p` is a useful minimal model for finite phase dynamics.

---

## Linear Propagation

Free light is modeled as a linear process because light in vacuum obeys superposition to high accuracy.

In the finite model:

```text
z_out = w * z_in mod p
```

For a pure phase rotation such as `w = i`, no amplitude growth is required. Rounding is minimal or absent in the idealized step.

That is the core difference:

```text
light-like process = linear, untrapped, one step per tick
```

---

## Timelessness And Spacelessness Of Light

From the point of view of the model, light is not something moving through a pre-existing space at a speed.

The fundamental statement is:

```text
one operation per tick
```

For a linear untrapped process, each tick is simply the next relation in the process chain. There is no internal detour, no stored cycle, and no rest frame.

This is the intended meaning of the "timelessness" of light:

- light does not accumulate internal loop time;
- it has no closed self-process that could define rest;
- it is pure propagation through the relational network.

And the intended meaning of "spacelessness":

- space is not a container that exists below the process;
- distance is an emergent relation between stable loop structures;
- a purely linear process is prior to that emergent spatial rendering.

---

## Matter As The Failure Of Pure Linearity

Matter begins when the process no longer remains a simple linear chain.

The key additions are:

- multiple inputs,
- local self-processing,
- self-coupling,
- finite precision,
- rounding-dominated closure.

In short:

```text
light = process that propagates
matter = process that gets caught in itself
```
